Privacy Policy

Data Stored Locally

This Inventory App stores data locally in your browser using localStorage. The app may save product records, category records, language preference, and cookie consent choice.

No External Data Transfer

This app does not send your inventory data to an external server and does not use third-party analytics. Data remains on the browser and device where it was entered.

Your Control

You can remove saved data at any time by clearing your browser localStorage for this website.
